Features

36 points

Ready for the law, point by point

36 checks in the dashboard: the scanner confirms some of them, the rest is a short to-do list with hints.

every week automatic scan

The scanner finds cookies and keeps the declaration current

The scanner walks your pages, collects the cookies and trackers, and refreshes the list every week. When a new service appears on the site, you read about it in the dashboard.

every answer in the log

Consent you can prove

Every visitor answer is stored in case you are ever asked. Log entries cannot be changed after the fact.

34 languages

Your words, your company

Your company details, the address for requests and the links to your policies appear in the banner and in the settings window. On paid plans you can set your own text in any of the 34 languages; the rest the visitor sees in their own.

1 page

A cookie declaration on a public page of its own

The list of cookies, with what each one is for and how long it is kept, lives on its own page. The link goes in the site footer and in the banner settings window.

10 minutes to install

Installs on any site in 10 minutes

A site builder, WordPress, a shop, a tag manager or plain HTML. The dashboard shows straight away what the visitor will see.

And also

Trackers stay silent until asked

Analytics and ads do not start until your visitor presses a button.

Your ads keep working

A refusal does not switch Google off entirely: it counts without cookies and without identifying the visitor.

The dashboard in three languages

The interface, the mail and the scanner’s hints come in Russian, Romanian and English. Your visitor sees the cookies in their own language.

A team with roles

Colleagues and contractors work on the site under their own addresses: an editor configures, an observer only looks.

How it works

  1. 1

    Paste the banner line

    One line in the head: in your builder settings, in the site template or through a tag manager. No build step, no dependencies.

  2. 2

    Press “Check”

    The dashboard finds the line on your site: that both verifies the domain and confirms the install.

  3. 3

    Run a scan

    The scanner walks the site, finds third-party services and cookies and offers a ready-made table for the banner.

  4. 4

    Adjust the banner

    The first version publishes itself. Adjust the colours, texts and table in the dashboard if you want to.

Why not a free plugin

A free plugin will show a banner too, and for many sites that is enough. ConsentKit is built for Moldova: readiness for Law 195/2024 is broken down into 36 points in the dashboard, on paid plans Romanian and Russian sit in one banner, support is local, and the invoice for your accountant arrives by email. And everything is set up in a separate dashboard, not in your site’s admin panel.
