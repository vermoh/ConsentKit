A cookie consent banner you can install in ten minutes

Most banners only draw the buttons. On those sites the trackers start straight away, whatever the visitor presses. EU rules require otherwise.

ConsentKit asks your visitor first and starts the trackers and ads only afterwards. It speaks 34 languages on its own and records every answer, so you have something to show if you are ever asked.

How it works

  1. 1

    Add the banner line

    Copy one line from the dashboard and paste it at the top of your site. It works on Tilda, WordPress and any other site.

  2. 2

    Click Verify

    The dashboard visits your site and checks the line is there. That also proves the site is yours.

  3. 3

    Run a scan

    We walk your pages and find every tracker and cookie you are running. You get back a ready-made list for the banner.

  4. 4

    Adjust the banner

    The banner already works and the first cookie list publishes itself. After that you can change the colours and wording to match your site.

Features

Trackers stay quiet until the answer

Almost every tracker and ad stays off until your visitor presses a button. That is exactly what the rules require, and exactly what decorative banners never do. The rare exceptions are found for you, with instructions on what to fix.

Cookie scanner

You do not have to remember what is running on your site. We walk the pages, find every tracker and cookie, and write the list for the banner.

Consent log

If anyone asks how you can prove consent, the answer is already saved. We record the time and what the visitor chose, and on paid plans you can download the whole log as a file.

34 languages

Visitors see the banner in their own language and you translate nothing. Leave it as it is, or write your own wording.

Your ads keep working

A refusal does not switch your advertising off completely: Google still gets a proper signal and keeps counting, just without personal data. There is nothing for you to set up.

WordPress, GTM, Tilda

It installs on whatever your site is built with. WordPress has a plugin, and everything else takes one line you can paste even on free Tilda.

Pricing

A plan covers your organisation, not a single site. Moving to a cheaper plan never breaks anything: the banner keeps working, automatic scans stop, and the attribution line comes back.
