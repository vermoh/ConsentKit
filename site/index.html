How it works

  1. 1

    Add the banner line

    Copy one line from the dashboard and paste it at the top of your site. It works on Tilda, WordPress and any other site.

  2. 2

    Click Verify

    The dashboard visits your site and checks the line is there. That also proves the site is yours.

  3. 3

    Run a scan

    We walk your pages and find every tracker and cookie you are running. You get back a ready-made list for the banner.

  4. 4

    Adjust the banner

    The banner already works and the first cookie list publishes itself. After that you can change the colours and wording to match your site.

Features

Trackers stay silent until asked

Analytics and ads do not start until your visitor presses a button.

The scanner finds your cookies

We walk your pages and build the cookie list for the banner.

Consent you can prove

Every visitor answer is stored in case you are ever asked.

The banner speaks 34 languages

Visitors see their own language and you translate nothing.

Your ads keep working

Fits any site

Who it is for

Tilda sites

Tilda gives you nowhere to upload files, so we hand you the whole block instead. Paste it into “HTML code for HEAD” and it works.

WordPress and WooCommerce

It installs like any other plugin and is configured in the admin. Site speed-up plugins do not get in its way.

Online shops

A refusal does not wipe out your statistics and advertising. Google keeps counting your orders, just without personal data.

Agencies

Every client site sits in one dashboard. Scans run by themselves, and you get an email when a new tracker shows up on any of them.
