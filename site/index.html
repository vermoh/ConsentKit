How it works

  1. 1

    Paste the banner line

    One line in the head — in your builder settings, in the site template or through a tag manager. No build step, no dependencies.

  2. 2

    Press “Check”

    The dashboard finds the line on your site: that both verifies the domain and confirms the install.

  3. 3

    Run a scan

    The scanner walks the site, finds third-party services and cookies and offers a ready-made table for the banner.

  4. 4

    Adjust the banner

    The first version publishes itself. Adjust the colours, texts and table in the dashboard if you want to.

Features

Trackers stay silent until asked

Analytics and ads do not start until your visitor presses a button.

The scanner finds your cookies

We walk your pages and build the cookie list for the banner.

Consent you can prove

Every visitor answer is stored in case you are ever asked.

The banner speaks 34 languages

Visitors see their own language and you translate nothing.

Your ads keep working

A refusal does not switch Google off entirely: it counts without cookies and without identifying the visitor.

Installs on any site

A site builder, WordPress, a shop, a tag manager or plain HTML.

Your words, your company

Your company details, the address for requests and the links to your policies — in the banner and in the settings window. Your own text in any of the 34 languages.

The dashboard in three languages

Russian, Romanian and English — the interface, the mail and the scanner's hints. Your visitor sees the cookies in their own language.

A team with roles

Colleagues and contractors work on the site under their own addresses: an editor configures, an observer only looks.

Ready for the law, point by point

36 checks in the dashboard: the scanner confirms some of them, the rest is a short to-do list with hints.

Who it is for

Sites on builders

There is nowhere to upload files, so we hand you the whole block instead. Paste it into the field for code in the head — one paste, no plugins.

WordPress and WooCommerce

The plugin installs by copying a folder and is configured in the admin. It works with caching plugins too: the banner does not depend on PHP.

Agencies and your own sites

Many sites in one dashboard, access for colleagues with roles, scheduled scans and alerts about new trackers. Terms by agreement.
