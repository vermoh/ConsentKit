How it works

  1. 1

    Paste the banner line

    One line in the head — in your builder settings, in the site template or through a tag manager. No build step, no dependencies.

  2. 2

    Press “Check”

    The dashboard finds the line on your site: that both verifies the domain and confirms the install.

  3. 3

    Run a scan

    The scanner walks the site, finds third-party services and cookies and offers a ready-made table for the banner.

  4. 4

    Adjust the banner

    The first version publishes itself. Adjust the colours, texts and table in the dashboard if you want to.
